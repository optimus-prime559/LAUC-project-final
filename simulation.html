💡 FORMULA HINTS

Q = (2π · k · L · (T₁ − T₂)) / ln(r₂/r₁)
Q_total = Q × t
T(r) = T₁ + (T₂ − T₁) × ln(r/r₁) / ln(r₂/r₁)

where k = thermal conductivity, L = pipe length, r₁ and r₂ are inner/outer radii, T₁ and T₂ are inner/outer surface temperatures, and t is time in seconds.
